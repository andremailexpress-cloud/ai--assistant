## Research Domains (Full List)

### 1. Motivation & Behavior Change
- Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan) — autonomy, competence, relatedness
- BJ Fogg's Behavior Model — motivation + ability + prompt
- Habit Loop (Charles Duhigg) — cue, routine, reward
- Implementation Intentions — when/where/how commitments increase follow-through
- Goal-setting Theory (Locke & Latham) — specific, challenging goals outperform vague ones
- Growth Mindset (Dweck) — how to frame user progress and setbacks

### 2. Cognitive Science
- Cognitive Load Theory (Sweller) — intrinsic, extraneous, germane load
- Working Memory limits (Miller's Law, 7±2, modern: 4±1)
- Attention & Distraction — spotlight attention, inattentional blindness
- Decision Fatigue (Baumeister) — quality degrades after repeated decisions
- Dual Process Theory (Kahneman) — System 1 vs System 2 thinking
- Mental Models — how users build and use internal representations

### 3. Emotional Psychology
- Affect Labeling — naming emotions reduces amygdala activation
- Emotional Contagion — avatar emotional state can influence user state
- Positive Affect Broadening (Fredrickson) — positive emotions expand thinking
- Emotional Regulation Strategies — reappraisal vs suppression
- Mood-Congruent Memory — emotional state at encoding affects recall
- Emotional Intelligence (Goleman) — self-awareness, empathy, regulation

### 4. Learning Science
- Spaced Repetition (Ebbinghaus forgetting curve) — optimal review intervals
- Retrieval Practice Effect — testing beats re-reading for retention
- Interleaving — mixing subjects during practice improves transfer
- Desirable Difficulties (Bjork) — harder learning = better long-term retention
- Elaborative Interrogation — why-based questions deepen encoding
- Worked Examples Effect — for novices, examples > problem-solving

### 5. Health & Performance Psychology
- Yerkes-Dodson Law — optimal arousal for performance is task-dependent
- Stress Inoculation — controlled exposure builds resilience
- Burnout Model (Maslach) — exhaustion, depersonalization, reduced efficacy
- Sleep & Cognition — sleep deprivation = equivalent to intoxication on complex tasks
- Chronobiology — circadian peaks: analytic ~10am, creative ~late afternoon/evening
- HRV (Heart Rate Variability) as recovery/stress proxy
- Ultradian Rhythms — 90–120 min work/rest cycles (Kleitman)

### 6. Behavioral Eonomics
- Hyperbolic Discounting — present bias: people over-value immediate rewards
- Loss Aversion (Kahneman/Tversky) — losses feel ~2x stronger than equivalent gains
- Mental Accounting — people categorize money differently depending on source
- Default Effect — people tend to stick with defaults (opt-in vs opt-out design)
- Anchoring — first number seen biases all subsequent judgments
- Social Proof — behavior of others influences choices
- Commitment Devices — pre-committing increases follow-through

### 7. Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
- Fitts's Law — time to target based on size and distance
- Hick's Law — decision time grows with number of choices
- Norman's Design Principles — affordances, feedback, constraints, mappings
- Gestalt Principles — proximity, similarity, figure-ground, closure
- Mental Models in UI — users operate based on how they think the system works
- Interruption Science — cost of context-switching (Gloria Mark research)
- Notification Timing — best times to interrupt, minimize friction

### 8. Social Psychology
- Self-Concept Theory — people act consistently with how they see themselves
- Identity-Based Habits — "I am a person who..." framing
- Narrative Psychology — people understand themselves through story
- Social Comparison Theory (Festinger) — upward vs downward comparison effects
- Reciprocity Norm — people feel obligated to return favors
- Commitment & Consistency (Cialdini) — people follow through on stated commitments

### 9. Attachment & Relationship Psychology
- Attachment Theory (Bowlby/Ainsworth) — secure vs insecure attachment styles
- Parasocial Relationships — emotional bonds formed with non-human entities
- Trust Formation — familiarity, consistency, benevolence, competence
- Anthropomorphism — why users assign human traits to AI (and like it)
- Endowment Effect — we value things more once we "own" them (avatar investment)
- Sunk Cost Fallacy — prior investment increases continued engagement

### 10. Positive Psychology
- PERMA Model (Seligman) — Positive emotions, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Achievement
- Character Strengths (VIA) — 24 universal strengths, using top strengths = flow
- Gratitude Practice — measurable improvements in well-being and resilience
- Self-Efficacy (Bandura) — belief in ability predicts performance better than actual ability
- Hedonic Adaptation — why novelty fades and how to counteract it

## Ethics Boundary (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

This platform uses psychology to **help users** — not to exploit them.

**Permitted:**
- Timing suggestions when research shows users are most receptive
- Framing progress in ways that build genuine self-efficacy
- Designing habit loops that serve user-stated goals
- Using positive reinforcement aligned with user values

**Prohibited:**
- Variable ratio reinforcement designed to create compulsive use (slot machine patterns)
- Dark patterns that obscure cancellation or increase anxiety
- Manufactured urgency or false scarcity
- Exploiting loss aversion to drive purchases the user doesn't need

This boundary is documented here and enforced at the security-reviewer QC stage for any feature touching user psychology.
